Privacy Policy — SPADA UPNYK Assignment Scanner

Last updated: 2026-11-19

SPADA UPNYK Assignment Scanner is an unofficial student productivity tool designed to scan active course assignment pages on https://spada.upnyk.ac.id/ and synchronize assignment deadlines to your personal Google Calendar.

1. What Data Is Accessed and Processed

  • Course & Assignment Content (spada.upnyk.ac.id): Extracts active course names, assignment titles, due dates, assignment URLs, and submission statuses from your signed-in SPADA session.
  • Preferences & Scan Cache: Stores your chosen calendar ID, time/status filters, excluded course IDs, reminder presets, event colors, and latest scan results locally in your browser (chrome.storage.local).
  • Google OAuth Access Tokens & Writable Calendar List: Requests https://www.googleapis.com/auth/calendar.events when you connect/sync to write assignment events to your chosen Google Calendar, and optionally requests https://www.googleapis.com/auth/calendar.calendarlist.readonly in Options to read writable calendar names and IDs on-device for destination selection. Only your chosen calendar ID is stored locally; tokens are never persisted in storage.

The extension never reads, stores, or transmits your SPADA username, password, or browsing history outside spada.upnyk.ac.id.

2. How Data Is Stored and Transmitted

All scanned data and preferences (including your selected target calendar ID) stay locally on your device. No developer-operated server receives your data. When Google Calendar sync is connected, assignment event details (titles, deadlines, reminders, color selections, and SPADA links) are sent directly from your browser to Google's official Calendar API (https://www.googleapis.com/calendar/v3/) on your selected Google Calendar. When authorized in Options, writable calendar names and IDs are read directly on-device solely to populate the destination calendar selector.

3. Third-Party Services & Data Sharing

We do not use analytics or telemetry, and we never sell or share user data with third parties. Google Calendar API usage is governed by Google's Privacy Policy.

4. Data Retention & User Controls

You can delete all locally cached scan data and settings anytime using the Hapus Data Lokal button in the extension's Options page, or uninstall the extension. You can revoke Google Calendar access anytime via the extension's disconnect button or Google Account Permissions.
